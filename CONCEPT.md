# Sharpen — the working concept

Scratchpad for developing the partner challenge. The preview in `index.html` is
the current state of it.

---

## The name

**Sharpen**, from Joe's own line: *as iron sharpens iron, so one friend sharpens
another.* It is a verb rather than a label, it sits beside "The Answer" without
competing with it, and it names the thing two people do rather than the container
they sit in.

Alternates considered: *The Forge* (heavier, and about heat rather than
friendship), *Iron* (blunt), *Circles* (names the container rather than the act).

**Open with Joe.** He is the final voice on copy, and this is question 1 in the
preview.

## The shape of the twenty-eight days

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | You pair with a six-character code. Each of you names one thing you are sharpening this cycle, and your partner is the one person who sees it. |
| **1–7 · Root** | Show up beside each other. Wordless. The shared number begins to climb. |
| **8–14 · Turn** | The honest week. The anchor writes to the circle on day 8. This is where a rest day usually lands. |
| **15–21 · Gather** | You send appreciation rather than a reminder. What each of you named on day 1 comes back into view. |
| **22–28 · Carry** | The number is high enough to mean something. |
| **Day 28** | The number is read back to you both, and your names are read aloud on the call. Then one choice: go again with the same person, or carry it to someone new. |

The movements are already defined in the main app as one constant (`MOVEMENTS`:
Root / Turn / Gather / Carry, on days 1–7 / 8–14 / 15–21 / 22–28). Sharpen gives
each of them a second person rather than inventing a new calendar.

## The line to keep checking against

> A member who finishes the month with a partner has done twenty-eight days
> beside someone. A member who finishes it alone has still done twenty-eight
> days.

The practice must be whole without a partner. If any part of Sharpen gates,
blocks, or diminishes the solo daily loop, that part is wrong.

## Open questions, in priority order

1. **The name.** Does Sharpen carry?
2. **The rest day.** "Todd is resting. Your circle keeps his place." Does that
   hold someone, or let them off? This is the single most load-bearing sentence
   in the design.
3. **Appreciation.** One tap sends a line. Does Joe write those lines, or does
   each member write their own? Joe-written keeps the voice and scales; a
   member's own words carry more.
4. **Circle size.** Eight comes from the ratio every durable small-group
   organization converged on. Confirm Joe would want to hold eight weekly.
5. **Whether Joe anchors a circle from day one.** He cannot design the anchor
   role without having done it, which argues yes.

## What stays out, deliberately

Leaderboards. Ranking. Streak ordering. Visible absence. Any way to nudge,
remind, or prod. A feed. A group chat as the first move. Members under 18.

## First real step

Three circles of eight, starting October 1 — the same day the daily text begins,
on the same phone number. What it asks for is people rather than infrastructure.
