Question two, continued

Four rules hold it steady.

Presence, and presence alone.
You see the day a friend showed up. A quiet day simply stays quiet: the screen holds his place and says so. Our own language rule already forbids the sentence that would announce a friend's absence, so the surface that would mean it stays unbuilt.
The shared number only rises.
A number two people can break becomes a number two people defend, and defending it invites a lie. Every app that shipped a breakable shared streak now charges its users to protect it, or removed the feature outright. This one climbs, and holds where it is.
Appreciation is the only thing you can send.
One tap sends a line in your voice. A way to nudge, remind or prod stays out of the design deliberately. You named appreciation yourself, and the research agrees: in twelve-step work, being the sponsor helps the sponsor more than the sponsee. The tap is built to help the person who taps.
The journal stays on the phone.
Every word a member writes remains on their own device. A first name, a date and a number are the only things that ever travel. The journal is where the honesty lives, and a member who suspects a reader writes for that reader instead.
